HueForge Floating Frame Maker

Enter your print's width and height, click Generate, download, print.
  • Print exactly as exported: frame back face down. No supports, no brim (a brim welds the stand in).
  • PLA, 0.16 or 0.20 mm layers, 3 walls, 15% infill.
  • Snap the stand out of its bay, then slide it into the two dovetails on the back of the spine, from the right when you look at the back. It leans the frame back 10°. Slide it out to hang the frame on the keyhole.
  • Glue the HueForge print onto the ledge with small dots of E6000 or gel CA.
Frame design by Michael Bernasconi. Runs entirely in your browser (works offline) using OpenSCAD (GPL-2.0, openscad.org, WASM build github.com/lorenzowritescode/openscad-wasm) and three.js (MIT).
